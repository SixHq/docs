Every plan includes the Sixth agent, all its tools, and access to the frontier models through your Sixth account. Plans differ in how much you can use and how fast.

<Note>Prices below are current launch prices. Check [app.trysixth.com/pricing](https://app.trysixth.com/pricing) for the latest, and to upgrade.</Note>

## Monthly and yearly plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | 50 agent / chat requests per month, 2,000 code completions per month, MCP, skills and hooks. No card required. |
| **Pro** | $10/mo | $8/mo (billed $100/yr) | Unlimited chat and code completions, extended agent limits on all frontier models, image generation, coding via Telegram. |
| **Pro+** | $30/mo | $22.50/mo (billed $270/yr) | Everything in Pro, 5× agent usage on Claude, GPT and Gemini, priority queue, 24/7 dedicated support. |
| **Ultra** | $100/mo | $83/mo (billed $1,000/yr) | Everything in Pro+, 20× usage on all models, early access to the newest models, dedicated infrastructure priority, white-glove onboarding. |

## Unlimited plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unlimited Yearly** | $1,250 per year | Zero usage caps and no rate limits for the whole year, every frontier model plus early access to new ones. |
| **Unlimited for Life** | $1,750, paid once | Unlimited access for life with no caps or rate limits, every current and future model, no subscription or renewals. |

## Upgrading

You can upgrade at any time from the pricing page, from your dashboard, or from the upgrade prompts in the extension. Upgrades take effect immediately.

When you move from a paid plan to **Unlimited Yearly** or **Unlimited for Life**, what you've paid for your current plan's billing period is credited toward the new plan, so you only pay the difference. For example, moving from a $100 Ultra month to Unlimited Yearly costs $1,150 instead of $1,250.

<Note>The credit only applies to an active, paid-up subscription. A subscription that's been canceled or has an unpaid invoice doesn't earn a credit.</Note>

## Payment

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, and you can cancel at any time from your dashboard. Unlimited for Life is a single one-time payment.
